# Hourline — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Hourline is a world clock and time zone converter. This policy explains what happens to your information when you
use the app.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Hourline does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal or usage data.

- **No account** is needed and none can be created.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking** tools are included in the app.
- **No location access.** Your home city is taken from your phone's time zone setting, which the app reads on the
  device without any permission.

## What stays on your phone

The cities you add, your home city and your settings (12/24-hour clock, theme) are saved **only on your device** so
the app remembers them. They never leave your phone. Uninstalling Hourline deletes them.

## Network use

Hourline works entirely offline. Its time zone rules are built into the app.

## Children

Hourline is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

World clock · converter · meeting planner

Every time zone, any moment.

Hourline shows the time in all your cities and gets daylight saving right, every time. Travel forward or back in time, find the hour that works for everyone, and see clock changes before they happen.

Join the closed beta Android · free · no ads, no tracking

Time travel

Drag the dial to any moment

Scrub the dial or tap a day to jump into the future or the past. When clocks change in between, Hourline says so, like “GMT · DST ended” for London, and moves every city with it.

Compare

Find the hour that works

An hour grid shows work, free and sleep hours for every city side by side, and picks the best overlap for your next meeting.

Convert

Convert any time, anywhere

Pick a date and time in one city and see it everywhere, including UTC. Swap cities in one tap. Pick a time that’s skipped when clocks spring forward and Hourline warns you instead of quietly guessing.

Clock changes

Never miss a clock change

See exactly when the next change happens, what time it happens locally, and how the gap between your cities moves afterwards.

Light & dark

Easy on the eyes, day or night

Light and dark themes that follow your phone, a 12- or 24-hour clock, and big, readable type that scales with your font size.

Accurate by design

Closed beta on Google Play

Help test Hourline

Hourline is in closed testing before it launches on Google Play. Join in three steps. It takes about two minutes.

  1. Join the tester group

    Sign in with the Google account you use in Google Play on your Android phone, then tap Join group.

    Join on Google Groups

  2. Become a tester

    Open the testing page with the same account and tap Become a tester.

    Open testing page

  3. Install Hourline

    Install it from Google Play like any other app. Updates arrive automatically.

    Get it on Google Play

Please keep Hourline installed for at least 14 days. Google needs that before the app can go public. Found a bug or have an idea? Email hello@adevtutorials.in.

Questions

Is Hourline free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones from Google Play. An iPhone version is planned.

What data do you collect?

None. The app works offline and your cities stay on your phone. Joining the beta goes through Google Groups and Google Play. See the privacy policy.

The testing page says the app isn’t available

Make sure you joined the group with the same Google account you use in Google Play, then reload the page. It can take a few minutes after joining.

How do I leave the beta?

Open the testing page and tap Leave the program, then leave the group from Google Groups.
